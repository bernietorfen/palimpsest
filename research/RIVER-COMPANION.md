# A River Twice

An illustrated companion to an original film, score and finite observation
instrument. The document starts with a human question: what does it mean to
recognize something when its relationships have changed?

## 1. What returns?

A familiar part can return while the whole remains different. The film turns
that tension into a woven architecture and a musical question that changes
carrier. The scientific instrument asks a narrower, testable question: when
do returning observations imply a returning state?

The images are authored geometry driven by a finite phase model. They are not
photographs of quantum matter. Feeling recognition, absence or recurrence is
an artistic invitation, not a measured perceptual result.

## 2. A fragment is not the whole

Four complex amplitudes evolve unitarily with energies
`(0,1,sqrt(2),1+sqrt(2))` and equal populations. The observer first reads only
pairs `(0,1)` and `(2,3)`. At time `2*pi`, both pair relationships return
exactly. The full pure-state trace distance from the beginning is nevertheless
`0.963902532850`, where zero means the same state and one means orthogonal.

Adding the relationship `(1,2)` exposes a difference that was already there.
The state is not changed by this choice of mathematical readout. In a physical
experiment, these quantities would be estimated from ensemble measurements;
the display is not disturbance-free monitoring of one unknown specimen.

The 32-mode extension repeats this construction with four groups of eight.
Every observed within-group relationship returns at `2*pi`, while the full
distance is `0.991221653246`. Hidden relationships have not been erased.

## 3. How a clock distinguishes moments

Write the readout as the vector of weighted complex coherences
`y_G(t)=(sqrt(a_jk)*rho_jk(t))`. Both real and imaginary parts matter; their
magnitudes alone are constant in this model. For separation delta,

    R_G(delta) = ||y_G(t+delta)-y_G(t)||^2
              = 4 sum a_jk p_j p_k sin^2((E_j-E_k)*delta/2).

The exact local coefficient is
`C_G=sum a_jk p_j p_k(E_j-E_k)^2`. With the analogous fourth-power sum K,

    C_G*delta^2 - K_G*delta^4/12 <= R_G(delta) <= C_G*delta^2.

Complete unit-emphasis observations give `C=Var_p(E)`. For the known pure
unitary family and hbar=1, quantum Fisher information for time is
`F_Q=4 Var_p(E)`. This established local quantum-estimation identity is not
the selected graph's classical measurement Fisher information or a guarantee
of knowing which distant moment produced a readout. [4,5]

## 4. Local sharpness, distant ambiguity

Choose a deterministic radius eta around each possible absolute readout.
Two candidate times allow the same report exactly when their readout distance
is at most `2*eta`. Their midpoint supplies an explicit overlap witness.
This is a chosen weighted-norm uncertainty model, not a physical shot budget
or a probability of making an error.

At eta=0.01, the matched-emphasis disconnected four-mode observer has a
finer local separation threshold than the connected observer: 0.04619213
versus 0.05429410. Yet the former retains every exact `2*pi` alias. The
connected observer rejects the first and admits a nonzero near-alias at
cycle 29. The complete observer first admits one at cycle 70 on the declared
grid. These are candidate-grid statements, not first ambiguities in continuous
time.

For 32 modes, the connected observer first admits a candidate at cycle 623.
The complete observer admits none among cycles 1 through 4096 for any
declared eta up to 0.05. Its closest recorded distance is 0.13582745. This
finite negative result does not exclude a later return or unsampled aliases.

The exact commensurate control `(0,1,2,3)` has larger quantum Fisher
information, 5 versus 3, yet the entire state returns every `2*pi`. A clock
can be locally sharp and still need an external cycle count. [1]

## 5. When a return can be certified

Let `z_j=exp(-iE_jt)`, `P=diag(p)` and let L have edge weights
`a_jk p_j p_k`. Populations are positive and fixed. For a connected graph,
lambda is the first positive eigenvalue of `P^(-1/2)L P^(-1/2)`.

Set `mu=sum p_j z_j`, `u=z-mu*1` and `x=P^(1/2)u`. Then x is orthogonal
to `sqrt(p)`, the null eigenvector, and

    D^2 = sum p_j |u_j|^2 = x^dagger x,
    R_G = u^dagger L u
        = x^dagger(P^(-1/2)L P^(-1/2))x >= lambda*D^2.

Hence `D^2<=R_G/lambda`. This is the weighted graph spectral inequality
applied to the declared phase family, not a new general theorem. All-pairs
unit emphasis gives the exact identity `R_complete=D^2`; unordered pairs
are counted once. A disconnected graph leaves relative component phases
unseen and has no such global certificate. [2,3]

For the certificate, use a separate symbol epsilon for the error norm in a
measured **change** vector:

    D <= min(1,(sqrt(R_measured)+epsilon)/sqrt(lambda)).

The ambiguity study instead bounds each candidate's absolute readout by eta.
If two independently noisy endpoint estimates each have norm error at most
eta, their difference has error at most `2*eta`; it must not silently be given
the smaller bound eta. Neither convention models quantum measurement shots.
A weak bridge can make lambda small and the certificate uninformative.

## 7. Two moments, one specimen

Now ask a different question. One quantum register is prepared in either the
known initial state or its state at `2*pi*n`, with equal probabilities. No
trusted external cycle count, correlated time label or retained history is
supplied. Such information can change the task.

At cycle 1, every effective measurement confined within the four groups has
success exactly 50%, while unrestricted optimal success is 99.5610826623%.
The restriction includes preprocessing; arbitrary intergroup mixing removes
it. The represented block state `E(rho)=sum P_g rho P_g` returns exactly,
but no physical dephasing is applied. The Holevo–Helstrom optimum is
`P_success=(1+D)/2`. [2]

The fixed integer construction found a full near return at cycle 4109,
beyond the earlier grid ending at 4096. For Q=128, indices 4110 and 1 share
box `(53,93,30)` and yield integer witness `(5811,7117,9188)` for the three
irrational phases. Exact square-root enclosures certify
`|4109*sqrt(s)-m_s|<1/128`, s=2,3,5. Comparing phase variance to reference
phase 1 gives `D<=pi*sqrt(3)/128<0.05`. This is an application of established
simultaneous approximation, not a new recurrence theorem. [6]

An independent 80-digit calculation gives `D=0.017388884873856538` and
unrestricted optimal success 50.8694442437%. Restricted success remains
exactly 50%. This is one constructed witness, not the first recurrence.
More independent copies or an external record can add information. This
single-copy task is separate from the earlier ensemble-readout error balls.
The exact witness and full proof are in `OPERATIONAL-TIME-RESULTS.md` and
the saved `operational-time-001/run-002` record.

## 8. A return depends on its context

The near return at cycle 4109 assumes an isolated clock. A separate
32-by-2 extension adds one environment qubit with a weak commuting phase
interaction, `chi*A tensor Z`, where `chi=1/10000` and the four group values
of A are `(-3,-1,1,3)`. Every within-group block still follows its isolated
evolution. The Hamiltonian has changed; this extension was not used to
generate the film. [7]

At that cycle, the isolated clock distance is 0.017388885. A coherent pure
environment gives clock distance 0.891417417 and joint entanglement entropy
0.946660194 bits. A maximally mixed environment produces exactly the same
clock state, but an explicitly separable joint state. Clock observations
alone cannot tell these mechanisms apart. [8]

The eigenstate environment is a decisive control: the joint state remains
a product, yet the clock distance is even larger, 0.929475066. Entanglement
is not necessary to disrupt this return. A weak phase coefficient can
accumulate a large change over a distant interval.

Returning purity also falls short of returning the joint state. At cycle
2500 the primary preparation is unentangled again, but the environment is
orthogonal to its initial state. At cycle 5000 the interaction is exactly
undone, while the clock still has distance 0.753341268 from its beginning.
Neither event is a joint recurrence. All 18 cases passed a separately
authored 80-digit operator review.

This is finite, jointly unitary phase dynamics. It establishes neither a
thermal bath nor irreversible loss. The scope and complete control record
are in `CLOCK-ENVIRONMENT-RESULTS.md`. Snapshot discrimination uses known
states, one copy, equal priors and no external time record; mixed marginals
use density-matrix trace distances, not the earlier pure-state shortcut.

## 9. Limits, evidence and reproduction

The original clock models use known pure states and fixed populations;
the coupled extension can have mixed clock marginals. Ensemble
expectation-value uncertainty and single-copy snapshot discrimination are
separate operational tasks. They establish neither general mixed-state
tomography nor backaction-free observation. No dissipative arrow,
irreversible information loss, macroscopic history or literal model of human
memory is claimed. An exact return of a closed
total state also returns every included record, by contraction under partial
trace. [2]

The four passing records contain 61, 658, 169 and 1172 checks: 2060 in total.
There are 49 focused Python tests across the recorded runs; no new aggregate
test run is implied. Dense propagation, density-matrix
eigenvalues, explicit pair operators and joint basis changes independently
check the direct phase calculation. The second record includes 450 dense
local cases, midpoint witnesses, all 4096 candidate differences, declared
borderlines and finite negative results. The third contains the exact integer
recurrence witness, original high-precision phases and actual optimal

fourth study adds six times and three environment preparations, exact
