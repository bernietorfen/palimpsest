# Originality and provenance

## Scope

PALIMPSEST is an original audiovisual artwork and an authored mathematical instrument. It does not claim to be the first use of sound-responsive sculpture, wave dynamics, hysteresis, modal synthesis, or audiovisual feedback. The creative and technical contribution must be stated at the level actually implemented and verified.

## Authored here

The implemented contribution is a particular closed loop between two retained material fields and twelve authored voices. Signed rest-shape memory and accumulated fatigue change voice tuning; the changed pitches advance the phase of spatial patterns through which a delayed velocity response acts back on the material. The formulas, update order and limitations are specified in `MODEL.md` and `studio/material.py`.

The six-movement, 432-second score is authored in `studio/score.py`. Its five-gesture question returns 352 seconds later with identical input timing and strength. The camera repeats its original path too. The synthesis uses eight inharmonic partials per voice, a smaller ringing component, fatigue-dependent spectral shading, newly accumulated wear as quiet abrasion, and an original deterministic synthetic room. No recorded music or sampled instruments are used.

The selected visual form is a twice-rolled open sheet. Its radius is embossed by displacement and retained inscription; fatigue determines an authored clipping pattern. Custom geometry closes the resulting surfaces with front, back and boundary walls. The pigment, contour ink, lighting, depth of field, camera sequence and compositions were written for this work. The apparent openings are a visual mapping, not a fracture-mechanics result.

The completed edition contains a 4K, 10-bit film, stereo soundtrack, three 80-megapixel plates, three textured glTF/STL states, an 18-page illustrated notebook and an exhaustive atlas of 120 writing orders. The viewing room includes an original WebGL sculpture renderer and an audible comparison of the recorded histories.

The exhaustive experiment preserves an identical gesture multiset while testing every order. At the 1/96-second timestep, the smallest pairwise pitch-trajectory difference is 0.171444 Hz RMS. Halving the timestep preserves the nearest matching history in 120/120 cases. The 60 pairs with the same final three gestures remain distinct as well. These are finite-grid numerical results; they do not establish universal historical priority, robust information capacity, or audible discrimination of every pair. All probe audio uses the same synthesizer, gain and room; the fully erased and fresh PCM controls match exactly.

## Attribution boundary

Runtime libraries and rendering/encoding tools are infrastructure. Their use does not make their implementations original to this work. Python, PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, ModernGL/OpenGL, Pillow, FFmpeg, Matplotlib, trimesh and ReportLab provide general tools. DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans provide the type; their license accompanies the self-hosted web fonts. The visual and musical assets are generated by this project's authored rules. No external images, video, music, or trained-model outputs are used.

## First precedent check, 3 October 2026

The general concept of history-dependent musical feedback is established. Tom Mudd's *Following the Material: Hysteresis, Intermittency and Timing-dependent Behaviour in Musical Interactions* explicitly discusses this territory (https://echo.orpheusinstituut.be/article/following-the-material). Sergey Filatov and Boris Shershenkov's *Hysteresis* is an earlier electroacoustic project using self-made sound objects (https://www.shershenkov.com/hysteresis). Alan Ahued Naime's *Plasticity of Sound* explores sculpture and nonlinear listening time (https://www.alanahuednaime.com/plasticity-of-sound).

These precedents prevent claims that memory, feedback, or sound sculpture are themselves inventions of PALIMPSEST. No code, presets, images, sound recordings, or compositions were obtained from these works. The specific toroidal material equations, delayed shifted-mode coupling, score gestures, geometry, shaders, and tests in studio/ were authored for this work. Those constitute an original implementation and composition; that alone does not establish a new scientific category.
