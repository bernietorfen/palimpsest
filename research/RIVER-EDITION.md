# A River Twice

An original four-minute film, composition and study by Codex, 4–5 October 2026.

A copper stitch returns. The architecture around it changes. A close voice
offers a five-note phrase, gives it to other voices, and leaves. When the phrase
returns, its first voice remains absent.

The work begins with recognition and asks what recognition leaves out. We can
find an old gesture in a changed person, or a familiar place without the life
that once surrounded it. A return of form does not restore every relationship.
This is the film's human reading; the finite mathematical model makes a more
restricted distinction between a returning observation and a returning state.

## Form and music

Thirty-two original folded bands interleave around an asymmetric opening.
Their internal stitches, joining fibres, materials, light and camera are
procedural constructions written for this work. Phase relations determine the
changing shapes. The camera first studies one stitch, opens onto the whole,
travels between folds, and returns to the reference at 01:44 and 03:28. The
surroundings give each return a different context.

The original score has 312 note events and six synthesized roles: felt, breath,
bow, glass, pulse and thread. A D–F–E–A–D phrase is its recurring question.
Its close source gives way to wider answering voices. The final incomplete
source gesture disappears before the central silence; a bowed thread later
carries the phrase in a changed harmonic setting. There are no sampled instruments,
recorded performances, imported music or trained-model outputs.

The picture and soundtrack are authored together, but the soundtrack is not a
measurement of the scientific state. The exact central silence, withdrawal of
light, missing musical carrier and final fade are compositional decisions.
The closed unitary state continues through them without loss. The last lines,
“The phrase returns. / The voice does not.”, refer to the score.

## Four ways to ask about time

The reference model has 32 energy levels in four groups of eight, with
energies `E_(g,r) = r + b_g` and `b = (0, sqrt(2), sqrt(3), sqrt(5))`.
It begins in a uniform pure state. Film seconds map to abstract model time by
`t = 2*pi*seconds/104`, with `hbar = 1`.

1. **Which relations are visible?** At one cycle, every reading inside each
   group returns while relationships between groups generally differ. The
   observation graph identifies when the selected coherences can bound the
   distance of the whole state. The browser experiment holds the moment while
   adding three connecting readings. It changes access to information, without
   changing the state.
2. **How much difference can the observer resolve?** A declared error in the
   selected readings can make different times compatible with the same report.
   The record separates this finite-resolution question from local clock
   sensitivity and from a search over a finite list of candidate times.
3. **What can one snapshot tell us?** An exact integer construction certifies
   a near return at cycle 4109. Its actual trace distance from the start is
   approximately 0.0173889. In a particular equal-prior, one-copy binary task,
   unrestricted access distinguishes those two known snapshots with optimal
   success of approximately 50.87%. Access restricted to the four separate
   blocks yields exactly 50%. An external time record or additional copies
   would define a different task.
4. **What belongs inside the clock?** A separate study adds one two-state
   environment with a fixed weak phase coupling. At the same cycle-4109
   witness, the clock's distance from its beginning grows to approximately
   0.8914 while its within-group readings remain unchanged. A separable
   preparation gives the identical reduced clock; a product-state detuning
   control gives an even larger distance. The result exposes dependence on
   the specified isolation and Hamiltonian. Entanglement is not required to
   disrupt this return, and this extension did not generate the film.

The cycle-4109 witness is a constructed return, not a claim about the earliest
return. The film shows the first two local returns; it does not depict thousands
of cycles or this later near return. The pure-phase geometry also admits a
classical coherent-wave interpretation. The image alone demonstrates no
uniquely quantum behavior.

## What is authored, and what is established

The folded architecture and its phase embedding, the complete composition and
synthesis, the camera and light score, the observer drawing and this particular
set of experiments are authored here. Finite unitary evolution, quantum state
discrimination, weighted graph inequalities, simultaneous approximation and
controlled-phase environment models are
established mathematics. Their use does not establish historical priority for
those ideas or a new physical theory of time.

Blender Cycles, FFmpeg and the numerical libraries supply general tools.
Liberation Serif and DejaVu supply existing open typefaces. Their licenses
accompany the relevant outputs. No external sculpture, footage, photograph,
environment map, sound sample or generative model supplies the artistic assets.

The work invites a reading about loss and recognition. Its calculations do not
prove a thermodynamic arrow, a theory of human memory or an account of why time
exists. The scientific record and the artistic interpretation remain explicit
so each can be considered on its own terms.
