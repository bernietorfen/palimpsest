The second act

A voice leaves.
Its encounter remains.

A gesture enters one vessel. Waves cross the connections and write into other bodies. The source leaves, the links are cut, and the same quiet question returns.

held in space.

The body that leaves

What gave
the most away.

Only B receives the writing contacts in this composition. Its own material changes as forces pass into the circle. Before it leaves, much of its visible skin has opened.

Its absence changes the composition. It does not erase the fields already retained by the others.

Inside the work

The encounter
can be tested.

A separate experiment writes only into a source, removes the connections and clears transient motion, then asks two untouched receivers the same question.

Every one of the 48 evaluated cases retains a changed reply. Erasing both retained fields removes the difference exactly. The notebook records the rules, controls, failed explorations and limits of that result.

A further question

Something of
the order arrives.

Four gestures touch the source in every possible order. Two bridges carry the encounter. After separation, the distant body answers differently to all twenty-four pasts.

Its reply at the finer timestep finds its own history in every case. The nearer body makes six different matches. That unexpected difference remains part of the work.

The print follows the recorded pitches. The experiment reads their full trajectories; it makes no claim that these small differences are audible.
