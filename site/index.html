This map draws selected readings, not physical connections. Changing the view does not change the clock.

The film uses a calculated 32-level unitary model in four groups. Its pure state evolves without loss. We calculate the real and imaginary parts of selected pair coherences: relations between two levels.

At t = 2π, corresponding to 01:44 in the film, all 28 readings inside the separate groups return to their starting values. Three additional readings between groups reveal a difference. They make a connected set of 31 selected pairs; there are 496 possible pairs in total.

For the full-state distance, 0 means the same state and 1 means perfectly distinguishable states. The limited readings can miss this difference.

Separate observation groups do not supply a global-distance bound.

For this known, fixed-population family, connected observations give D ≤ min(1, √(R/λ)) with exact calculated readings. A small graph gap can make the bound uninformative. The full distance displayed here is calculated independently from the model; it is not inferred from the drawing.

The curves use both coherence quadratures, divided by their fixed initial magnitude. Their placement and colors are authored. Selecting readings models no measurement backaction; it changes the observer's information, not the state. The same phase geometry also has a classical coherent-wave interpretation; the artwork does not demonstrate uniquely quantum behavior. The numerical record and exact return argument concern this finite model, not the nature of all time.

The images are controlled renders, not frames taken from the moving film. Within each pair, the camera and lighting are fixed. The close view isolates the reference family; the wide view includes the whole geometric embedding. Inside the work

What remains
between us.

We recognize a gesture, a voice, a place. Recognition does not bring back every relation that once surrounded it.

A River Twice gives that thought a material and musical form. The reference folds return; their surroundings change. A source voice leaves the composition, while the underlying phase clock continues through the silence.

The geometry, six synthesized instrument roles, score and camera are authored for this work. Musical withdrawal is an artistic choice. The closed scientific model does not lose its state.
