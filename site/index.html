The work

The question returns.
The material answers
from its history.

A phrase presses into an invented material. Its deformation leaves an inscription; its wear changes the tuning. The changed voices send a different force back into the surface.

For a while, writing and answering become the same act. Then the inscription is allowed to fade. The phrase returns to a surface that has already lived through it.

An original score, numerical instrument, film and series of sculptural states.

01 / The returning question

Same phrase.
Changed instrument.

Five gestures repeat with the same timing and strength. Compare their first appearance with their return, 352 seconds later. The camera repeats too.

Press play, then switch which instrument you hear.

Both clips contain instrumental synthesized tones without speech. Five gestures occur at the same times in both clips. The returning instrument has accumulated fatigue and a different inscription, changing its tuning. The pale intact sheet in the first clip has become a worn, open sculpture in the return.

02 / Material states

A score you can
walk around.

The same recorded fields become a twice-rolled sheet. Inscription embosses its surface. Wear opens windows through it. Each state can be turned and examined in three dimensions.

Digital sculptures from the performance. The openings are an authored image of wear.

03 / A study of remembered order

120 possible
pasts.

Five gestures can be written in 120 different orders. The same question, asked afterward, leaves 120 different recorded answers.

Choose the writing order. Hear two histories together, switch between them, and follow the marks each one leaves.

04 / Inside the instrument

The rules.
The record.
The evidence.

What changes when only the order of writing changes? The exhaustive study records all 120 histories at two timesteps. Their answers differ. Erasing both retained fields restores the fresh answer exactly.

The illustrated notebook follows the work from its equations to its final images, including the tests, interventions and limits of that result.
