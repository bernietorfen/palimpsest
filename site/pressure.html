    The experiment

    The hand changes.
    The material stays.

    Five gestures, A–E, are written into fresh material in each of their 120 possible orders. Each gesture receives its own random pressure multiplier. The timing is fixed. After writing, the moving fields are reset; the retained shape and wear remain. Then every material receives the same fourteen-second probe.

    Each answer contains 336 samples of twelve voice pitches: 4,032 numbers, including voices not being directly excited. The first reader chooses the nearest ideal answer by the root mean square difference across that whole record.

    The second reader has five extra pieces of information for each possible history: how its ideal answer changes when each gesture is pressed a little harder or softer. It allows a local linear adjustment along those five directions, then chooses the history with the smallest remaining difference. That adjustment is unbounded; it is a reading rule, not a measurement of the original pressure.

    Independent cases, declared rules

    The reading rule was fixed before these cases were generated. Its pressure directions came from separate controlled writes. It receives the answer alone, with no true history, actual pressure or severity supplied.

    At the largest variation, the correction recovers 516 histories and loses 9 that the first reader got right. Its remaining 79 errors are available to inspect above. Halving the timestep on the first trial of every history changes one of the 120 labels for each reader; the corrected reader identifies 108 of those 120 finer-step cases.

    This is a finite numerical experiment in an invented material. Timing errors, listening judgments and physical memory capacity were not tested. The pressure correction uses established least-squares geometry; the material, gestures, measurements and presentation were authored for this work.

    A map is a partial view

    The two plotted components retain 65.5% of the variation among the ideal histories. Points that look close here can differ elsewhere in the answer. Both reading rules use all 4,032 values. Switching readers changes labels, never the plotted measurements.

    Four moments, twelve directions

    The rings show the start of the probe, then 4, 8 and 12 seconds. Each direction is one voice. Outward means a pitch above the ideal histories’ average at that moment; inward means below it. All cases and references share one scale. The smooth curves connect measured values.
