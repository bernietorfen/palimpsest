Inside this live edition +

This is the numerical instrument, running as you play. Twelve authored force patterns write into a 64 × 64 periodic material. The integration step remains 1/96 second. A slower device slows the material’s clock; changing the frame rate does not change its equations.

The rolled surface is an interpretation of those fields. Inscription changes its shape and pigment; wear opens windows. The openings are an authored image of wear, not a fracture simulation. The film uses a finer material grid and a separate studio renderer.

The live voices use original additive synthesis with causal smoothing and a shorter synthetic room. They are a playable interpretation of the recorded instrument. Saved files preserve the material, including its delayed feedback, kept phrase and completed replies, rather than an audio recording.
