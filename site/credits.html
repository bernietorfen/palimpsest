Authorship &
acknowledgments

PALIMPSEST was conceived, coded and composed by Codex on 3–4 October 2026. It is an invented mathematical instrument and an original audiovisual work.

Made for this work

The particular coupling of rest-shape inscription, fatigue, tuning and delayed spatial feedback; the control score and synthesis; the rolled-sheet geometry, pigment, shaders, camera score and compositions; the numerical experiments; and this viewing room were authored for PALIMPSEST.

No borrowed organisms, procedural presets, mesh assets, photographs, recordings or trained-model outputs are used.

Established ingredients

Wave dynamics, plastic rest shapes, hysteresis, Fourier analysis, additive synthesis, feedback music and sound sculpture have substantial histories. This work makes an original construction from explicit rules. It does not claim universal historical priority or physical material accuracy.

Contextual precedents include Tom Mudd’s Following the Material and Alan Ahued Naime’s Plasticity of Sound. They locate neighboring practices; their code, imagery, recordings and compositions were not imported.

Tools and type

Production uses Python, PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, ModernGL/OpenGL, Pillow, FFmpeg, Matplotlib, trimesh and ReportLab. General software and mathematical methods retain their respective authorship. Simulation, synthesis, rendering, analysis and browser verification ran on RunPod.

Typography uses DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans. The font license accompanies the self-hosted type files. The browser sculpture viewer is a small original WebGL renderer for the exported PALIMPSEST meshes.

The record

The illustrated notebook describes the equations, tests and limitations. Geometry closure and video decoding were checked. Sound was measured for duration, peak and loudness; a supported perceptual audition path was unavailable to the authoring assistant.

The work’s source, study records and media are preserved with checksum manifests. Its measured history effect concerns this deterministic finite-grid instrument, rather than a general result about biological or physical memory.
